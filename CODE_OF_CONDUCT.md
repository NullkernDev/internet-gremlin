# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Internet Gremlin to be a friendly, welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

The Gremlin can be grumpy. People here can't.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind, patient and constructive — especially with newcomers.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Give and gracefully accept feedback on work, not on people.
- Credit others' ideas and contributions.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other channel where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainer via the contact listed on [github.com/NullkernDev](https://github.com/NullkernDev). All reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is harmful, in proportion to the situation.
