## [1.0.0] — 2026-11-07

The Gremlin hatches.

### Added
- Manifest V3 extension for Chrome, Edge and other Chromium browsers; works fully offline.
- Original procedurally generated pixel-art mascot with 15 core expressions plus dizzy, asleep and “The Stare”.
- Priority-based behaviour engine with 12 moods, an inactivity ladder (look around → yawn → sleep → complain), reunion behaviours, tab counting, tab-tornado dizziness, scroll and click reactions, cursor dodging and shyness.
- 253 original dialogue lines across 60 situations, 28 inner thoughts and 12 sticky-note items, with rarity tiers, relationship tiers and per-line cooldowns.
- Relationship system: affection, trust, chaos, interactions, time together, visits, favourite activity, four tiers, neglect grudges and forgiveness.
- 17 secret behaviours with an in-app collection screen.
- Mini interactions: petting by rubbing, feeding (cookie, byte, bug), a reaction game, mood dice, and current thoughts.
- In-page menu, snack tray, speech bubbles, sticky notes, secret toasts, minimised “peek” mode, per-site hiding, fullscreen auto-hide.
- Popup with mood, relationship bars and quick actions; full settings page with the Gremlin-dex, secrets, privacy panel and data viewer.
- Animated onboarding: hatching, naming, controls, personality intensity, privacy.
- Light/dark themes, three sizes, mute (sound off by default), speech toggle, animation intensity, reduced-motion mode.
- Unit tests, a Playwright end-to-end test against a real browser, ESLint and strict TypeScript.
- Asset pipeline (icons, sky, expressions), promo art, stickers, and a recorded 30-second demo video + GIF with an original soundtrack synthesised in code.
- CI workflow, issue and pull request templates.
